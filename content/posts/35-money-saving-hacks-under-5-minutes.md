Not every money saving strategy requires a lifestyle overhaul.

Some of the most effective ones take less than five minutes to set up and continue saving you money every month with no further effort. Here are thirty-five of them.

## In Your Bank Account (Under 5 Minutes Each)

**1. Set up a round-up savings feature.** Most banking apps have this. Every transaction gets rounded up to the nearest dollar and the difference goes to savings. Activate it now.

**2. Open a high-yield savings account.** Takes five minutes online. Move your emergency fund there and earn four to five percent instead of near-zero at a traditional bank.

**3. Set up one automatic savings transfer.** Even twenty dollars per pay period. Set it to move the day after payday. Done. You will not miss it.

**4. Turn off overdraft protection.** Overdraft fees are expensive and overdraft protection makes it easy to spend money you do not have. Turn it off and let declined transactions be your spending signal instead.

**5. Check your bank fees.** If you are paying a monthly maintenance fee, call and ask to have it waived or switch to a free account. This one call takes three minutes and saves money every month indefinitely.

## On Your Phone Right Now

**6. Cancel one streaming service you barely use.** Open your subscriptions and cancel the one you watched least this month. Do it right now.

**7. Delete one shopping app.** The fewer shopping apps on your phone, the fewer opportunities for impulse purchases. Delete one today.

**8. Turn off push notifications from retail apps.** Every notification is a curated invitation to spend. Turn them all off in your phone settings.

**9. Download your bank's app if you do not have it.** Seeing your balance before you spend is one of the simplest ways to reduce impulsive spending.

**10. Set a weekly spending alarm.** Set a recurring Sunday alarm called check your spending. Five minutes of awareness weekly prevents months of regret.

## For Your Bills Right Now

**11. Call your phone provider and ask for a better rate.** Say you are considering switching. Most providers will discount your bill within the call.

**12. Set all your bills to autopay.** Eliminates late fees entirely for a one-time five-minute setup per bill.

**13. Check if you qualify for any discount on your utilities.** Many providers offer discounts for automatic payment, paperless billing, or low income customers. One phone call finds out.

**14. Cancel any free trial before it converts to paid.** Check your email right now for confirmation emails from free trials. Cancel everything you are not actively using.

**15. Switch to a cheaper phone plan.** Budget carriers like Mint Mobile often provide identical service at a fraction of the cost. A five-minute comparison can save fifty to one hundred dollars monthly.

## While Online Shopping

**16. Search for a coupon code before every checkout.** Open a new tab. Search the store name plus coupon code. Takes thirty seconds and frequently saves ten to twenty percent.

**17. Install a cashback browser extension.** Rakuten and Honey automatically find and apply discounts and earn cashback on purchases you were making anyway.

**18. Add items to your cart and wait 48 hours before buying.** If you still want them after 48 hours, buy them. Most impulse purchases evaporate before then.

**19. Turn off one-click purchasing on Amazon.** The friction of re-entering payment details gives you a moment to reconsider.

**20. Check if a library has the book before you buy it.** Your library's app shows ebooks and audiobooks available for free right now.

## In Your Kitchen

**21. Move older food to the front of the fridge.** You will use it before it expires rather than discovering it in the back after it has gone bad.

**22. Freeze bread before it goes stale.** Freezes and defrosts perfectly. Eliminates one of the most common food waste culprits.

**23. Switch to a reusable water bottle.** Five-minute purchase that eliminates bottled water spending permanently.

**24. Make a meal plan for the week.** Takes fifteen minutes on Sunday. Eliminates mid-week what is for dinner panic buying throughout the rest of the week.

**25. Store herbs in water like flowers in a vase.** They last two to three times longer, reducing fresh herb waste significantly.

## With Your Insurance

**26. Raise your deductibles if you have an adequate emergency fund.** Higher deductibles equal lower premiums. Takes one phone call to your insurer.

**27. Ask about every available discount on your car insurance.** Safe driver, multi-car, low mileage, good student, professional association. Many discounts exist that insurers do not volunteer.

**28. Shop your home insurance at renewal.** Takes twenty minutes to compare quotes online and can save hundreds of dollars per year.

**29. Bundle your home and car insurance with the same provider.** Multi-policy discounts are typically significant.

**30. Check your coverage levels.** You may be paying for coverage you do not need or amounts that have not been updated to reflect your actual situation.

## Miscellaneous Quick Wins

**31. Unsubscribe from every retail email list.** One morning, thirty minutes, unsubscribe from every store email. The temptation to spend comes significantly from promotional emails. Remove it.

**32. Switch your default search engine.** Microsoft Rewards pays points for searches via Bing that convert to gift cards. Takes two minutes to set up.

**33. Request a credit limit decrease if you have a spending problem.** A lower credit limit means less potential for credit card debt. Counterintuitive but effective.

**34. Check for unclaimed money in your name.** Search your name on your country or state's unclaimed property database. Takes five minutes and occasionally produces a genuine surprise.

**35. Review your paycheck withholding.** If you consistently receive a large tax refund, you are giving the government an interest-free loan. Adjusting your withholding puts that money in your hands monthly instead of annually.

Pick five from this list and do them today. Not this week. Today. Five minutes each. Done.
